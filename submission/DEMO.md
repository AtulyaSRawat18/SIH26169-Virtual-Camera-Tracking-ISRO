## Recommended 3-minute recording

1. **0:00–0:20 — Problem:** narrow FSOC beams require coarse camera alignment before fine steering.
2. **0:20–0:50 — Architecture:** show the modular loop and explain that truth is evaluation-only.
3. **0:50–1:35 — Live Demo:** run a `DEMO_*` scenario and show 3D motion, OpenCV localization, gimbal response and lock state.
4. **1:35–2:10 — Disturbance:** raise one physical error level and show the loop respond or reacquire.
5. **2:10–2:40 — Evidence:** show RMSE/P95, paired comparison, Monte Carlo sensitivity and replayable failure seeds.
6. **2:40–3:00 — USP:** reproducible software-in-the-loop development, fair comparisons and evidence-gated AI.

Do not cut away from failures. A short replayable failure plus diagnosis is stronger evidence than a scripted perfect run.
